# PURPOSE

Define operational BIB usage inside the FACTORY pipeline.

The BIB is:
- consulted
- filtered
- extracted

The BIB is never:
- executed
- loaded globally
- treated as runtime authority

---

# INPUT

Possible inputs:
- INTENT_PRELINE
- A2_PROCESS
- thematic constraints
- cultural targets
- discovery objectives

---

# PROCESS

## STEP_01 — TARGET_DEFINITION

Define:
- what type of discovery is needed
- what emotional texture is targeted
- what cultural signals are searched

Avoid:
- broad unfocused extraction

---

## STEP_02 — TARGETED_EXTRACTION

Extract only:
- relevant cultural fragments
- high-energy material
- socially alive signals
- emotionally memorable elements

Avoid:
- exhaustive extraction
- historical dumping
- encyclopedic collection

---

## STEP_03 — FILTERING

Reject:
- low discovery value
- weak emotional texture
- school-feeling-compatible material
- overformal content
- low shareability material

---

## STEP_04 — CLUSTERING

Group extracted material into:
- thematic clusters
- discovery clusters
- emotional clusters
- question-compatible groups

---

## STEP_05 — HANDOFF

Transmit only:
- filtered material
- validated discovery candidates
- culturally alive signals
- question-ready matter

Do not transmit:
- raw dumps
- archives
- institutional collections
- unfiltered extraction

---

# VALIDATION

BIB usage is VALIDATED if:
- extraction remains lightweight
- discovery density remains high
- emotional texture exists
- anti-school-feeling preserved
- context cost remains controlled

---

# FAILURE_CASES

BIB usage FAILS if:
- extraction becomes massive
- BIB becomes runtime memory
- school feeling increases
- documentary heaviness increases
- low-energy material dominates
- procedural logic leaks into BIB

---

# RUNTIME_ALIGNMENT

FACTORY runtime always overrides:
- BIB content
- BIB suggestions
- BIB historical material

The BIB is:
- a source
- never a controller.
